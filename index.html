Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every estimate is free. Tell us about your project by phone, email or the request form, and we'll provide a clear estimate before any work begins.

What areas do you serve?

We serve homeowners throughout New Jersey. If you're not sure whether we cover your town, give us a call and we'll let you know.

When should I schedule my spring start-up?

Spring start-ups are best scheduled once overnight freezing temperatures have passed. We'll turn your system on, check it zone by zone and adjust heads so everything runs properly for the season.

Why is winterizing my sprinkler system important?

Water left in the lines can freeze and expand during the winter, cracking pipes, valves and sprinkler heads. Winterizing clears the water from your system in the fall to help prevent costly repairs in the spring.

Can you find sprinkler valves that are buried or hidden?

Yes. Locating hidden or buried valves is one of our specialties. We'll find them and repair or replace them as needed.

Can you add zones or expand my existing system?

Yes. We handle all types of new additions, including adding zones and extending coverage to areas of your yard that aren't being watered.

I'm installing a pool. Can you move my sprinkler lines?

Yes. We relocate irrigation lines to make room for new pool installations, so your system keeps working around the new layout.

Do you offer services besides sprinkler systems?

Yes. In addition to irrigation, we provide fencing, drainage systems, landscape lighting, landscaping and lawn services, power washing and general property services.
