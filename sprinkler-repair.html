Signs Your Sprinkler System Needs Repair

  • Brown or dry patches in your lawn or garden beds
  • Puddles, soggy areas or water running down the driveway
  • Heads that don't pop up, spray crooked or mist instead of spray
  • A zone that won't turn on, or one that won't turn off
  • Low water pressure across one or more zones
  • A water bill that jumps for no clear reason

Catching problems early usually means a simpler, less expensive repair and less water wasted.

How a Repair Visit Works

We start by running your system zone by zone to see exactly what's happening. Then we explain what we found, what needs to be fixed and what it will cost before we start. Once you approve, we make the repair, test the system again and adjust your heads so every area is covered.

Every estimate is free, and you'll always know the price before work begins.
