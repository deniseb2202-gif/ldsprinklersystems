Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LD Sprinkler Systems LLC ("we," "us" or "our") collects, uses and protects the information you share with us through our website, by phone, by email and by text message.

Information We Collect

When you request a free estimate or contact us, we collect the information you choose to provide, including:

  • Your full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Zip code
  • The service you are requesting and any project details you share
  • Whether you agreed to receive text messages from us

If you call, text or email us directly, we also receive the information included in that communication, such as your phone number, email address and any photos or details you send.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to operate our business and serve you, including to:

  • Respond to your questions and estimate requests
  • Prepare and provide estimates
  • Schedule, perform and follow up on services
  • Send appointment reminders, service updates and account updates
  • Keep records of the work we perform

Text Messages

If you check the consent box on our estimate request form, you agree to receive transactional and informational text messages from us, such as appointment reminders and service and account updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or reply HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is collected only for these purposes.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run our business, for example:

  • With service providers that help us operate our website and receive form submissions, such as our form-delivery service (Web3Forms), which delivers your estimate request to our email inbox
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public

Our website also loads fonts from Google, and our website host may collect standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type as part of normal operation. We do not use our website to track you for advertising purposes.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to your request, provide services, maintain our business records and meet legal obligations.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, update or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by calling or emailing us using the contact details below. To stop text messages, reply STOP at any time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
