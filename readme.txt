LD Sprinkler Systems LLC - Website Files
==========================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html     Home page
  privacy.html   Privacy Policy
  terms.html     Terms of Service
  images/        All photos used on the site
  favicon.svg    The small LD icon shown in the browser tab
  robots.txt     Lets Google find and list the site

HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE (free, about 5 minutes)
  1. Unzip this file so you have a folder named "lds-sprinkler-website".
  2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and create a free Netlify account.
  3. Drag the whole "lds-sprinkler-website" folder onto the page.
  4. Netlify gives you a live address (something.netlify.app). Open it on your phone and test.

CONNECT YOUR OWN DOMAIN (for example ldsprinklersystems.com)
  1. Buy the domain from Namecheap, Cloudflare or GoDaddy.
  2. In Netlify: Site configuration > Domain management > Add a domain.
  3. Follow Netlify's steps to copy its settings into your domain provider's account.
     The secure "https" lock is set up automatically.

AFTER IT'S LIVE
  - Send yourself a test request from the estimate form and check it reaches
    ldsprinklersystemllc@gmail.com (check spam the first time, mark "Not spam").
  - Tap both phone numbers and the email links on your phone.
  - In your Web3Forms settings, restrict your access key to your new domain.
  - Create a free Google Business Profile (business.google.com) and add the website link.

TO UPDATE THE SITE LATER
  Make the change, then drag the updated folder onto your site's "Deploys" page in Netlify.
